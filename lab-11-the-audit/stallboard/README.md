# StallBoard

Price board for Rosa's Farm Stall (Saturdays, Stall 14). Shows what's on today, what it costs,
and what's sold out — Rosa taps "sold out" from her phone behind the stall, and the board
customers see from the queue stays true.

Open `index.html`. No install, no accounts, works offline at the market.

Built by Fern & Ledger Digital, June–July 2026. See `SPEC.md` for what "done" means,
`DECISIONS.md` for why things are the way they are, and `checks/` for the checklist.
