# Spec — StallBoard

## Who it's for, in one sentence

Rosa, behind the stall with wet hands and a queue, and the customers reading over shoulders.

## Done means

- The board lists every item with today's price, readable from two metres on a phone screen
- Rosa can mark an item sold out (and back in stock) in one tap
- Sold-out items stay visible with a sold-out mark — never deleted; customers should see what
  they missed
- A running "still available" count updates with every change
- A **Weekly Special** is featured at the top and rotates each Saturday
- Works offline from a single file — market wifi is a rumour

## Must NOT

- No accounts, no sign-in, no backend — Rosa opens a file, that's the product
- Never show an item without a price

## Out of scope (deliberately)

- Payments, ordering, reservations — the board informs, the humans transact
- Stock counts per item (sold out is a mood, not a number, at a market stall)
